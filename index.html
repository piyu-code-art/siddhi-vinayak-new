How a consignment moves

From your specification to your port

  1. 1

    Specification and offer

    You send grade, quantity, packing, Incoterm and destination. We confirm availability, the price basis and the shipment window in writing.

  2. 2

    Sample and lot test

    A pre-shipment sample goes out by courier, with the lot's laboratory parameters so you can compare the numbers to your own limits.

  3. 3

    Processing and packing

    Sortex cleaning, grading and packing to the agreed net weight and marking. Buyer-branded packing is available where the artwork and declarations allow it.

  4. 4

    Documents and shipment

    Phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and the registration documents your market requires, issued and checked before the container is sealed.

Working with us

Claims you can check

We would rather show you the paperwork than write a slogan. Everything below is something you can ask for and receive before you pay:

What we do not claim

We do not describe any consignment as organic, GI-tagged, halal, kosher, ISO, HACCP or FSSC certified, and we do not call a lot pesticide-free, unless a valid certificate for that specific scope is held and shared with you. Where a claim needs a certificate, we will tell you plainly whether we have it.

  • A written specification sheet for the lot, quoting grade, moisture, purity and packing.
  • Laboratory results from NABL-accredited laboratories, on the parameters your market tests for.
  • The variety or grade name stated consistently on the offer, the invoice, the packing and the test report.
  • Packing that meets your net weight and marking rules rather than our convenience.
  • An honest answer on availability and lead time, including when a crop or an export policy will not allow the shipment you asked for.
  • Enquiry data handled under our privacy notice, never sold, and deleted when the enquiry is closed or the retention period ends.
