Cookie policy

This site runs without advertising or cross-site tracking. What it stores in your browser, why, and how to change your choice at any time.

Last reviewed: 26 September 2026

What a cookie is here

Cookies and browser storage are small values a website asks your browser to keep. This site uses them only to remember your consent choice and to support the mobile menu state. There is no advertising cookie, no social pixel, and no third-party analytics on this site by default.

Managing your choice

  • The banner appears on your first visit: accept all, reject optional, or choose individually.
  • Change your mind at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of every page.
  • You can also delete stored values in your browser settings; the banner will then ask again.

Third parties

Pages load no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The Inter typeface, all illustrations and the logo are served from this site's own origin, so no external company sees your visit. If external media is ever embedded, it will stay blocked until you opt in through the consent panel.
