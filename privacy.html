Privacy notice

We ask for enquiry details so we can answer your enquiry, and nothing else. What we collect, why the law allows it, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Last reviewed: 26 September 2026

Summary

  • We collect only what you send via the enquiry form: name, company, email, phone, country, product, quantity, Incoterm and message.
  • We use it only to respond and to prepare an offer, sample or contract.
  • We never sell personal data and never use it for marketing without a separate request.
  • Closed enquiries are deleted within three years of the last message, or sooner on request.
  • You can seek access, correction, erasure or grievance redress at any time (see below).

Who we are

The data fiduciary for this website is SiddhiVinayak Exporters, India. Contact details are published on the about page and the contact page.

Lawful basis and consent

Processing rests on your free and informed consent given through the unticked checkbox on the enquiry form, read with this notice, as required by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights).

Sharing and transfers

No sale of personal data, ever. Operational sharing is limited to what the enquiry needs: the email provider carrying the message, or a legal authority where the law compels disclosure. We do not transfer data abroad except to reply to you at your foreign address.

How long we keep it

Open enquiries are kept while correspondence continues. Closed enquiries are deleted within three years of the last message, or sooner on request.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 you may ask for access, correction, erasure, nomination, and grievance redress. Contact:

  • ContactGrievance Officer / Data Protection contact
  • EmailUse the enquiry form and mark the message for the grievance officer.

We aim to acknowledge a request within seven days and complete it within thirty days. If you remain dissatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site offers export trade goods to businesses and is not directed at children. Data sent in error is deleted.

Changes to this notice

When this notice changes, the review date above is updated. Material changes are summarised here for six months.

Questions about your data?

Write through the enquiry form and mark the message for the grievance officer.
